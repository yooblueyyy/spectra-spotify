What you can change

Everything Spicetify can do, without the command line or a reinstall after every Spotify update.

  1. Themes, one click each

    The official set and hundreds of community themes, with previews. Apply one and Spotify changes on the spot.

  2. Colours you can tweak

    Switch between a theme's colour schemes, or change any single colour and watch the preview follow.

  3. Extensions that just run

    Lyrics, shuffle tools, keyboard shortcuts. Spicetify extensions run as they are, and one crashing doesn't take the rest down.

  4. Snippets and your own CSS

    A smaller now-playing bar, rounded album art, dancing hamsters. Stack small tweaks on any theme, or write CSS yourself.

  5. Survives Spotify updates

    Spotify renames its internals every few releases. Spectra spots the new names and fixes your theme by itself.

  6. Backups

    Export every theme, extension and setting to one file, and bring it to another computer.

Listen together

Start a room from the headphones button in Spotify. Everyone in it hears the same song at the same moment; pauses, skips and seeks follow the host, and anyone who drifts gets nudged back.

Make a room public so people can find it, or keep it private behind its six-letter code. The host can make friends DJs, who can queue songs and play playlists for everyone. Each person listens on their own Spotify account, browser, desktop or Quest.

Questions people ask

Is this Spicetify?

No, but it reads the same themes and runs the same extensions. Spicetify patches Spotify's files on your computer. Spectra changes Spotify while it's running, which is why it also works in the browser and doesn't break when Spotify updates.

Can my Spotify account get banned?

Spectra changes how Spotify looks and adds extensions on top. It doesn't touch ads, Premium or anything else account-related. That said, Spotify's terms don't officially allow modifying its apps, so there's no guarantee. Use it knowing that.

How does Listen Together work?

Click the Listen Together (headphones) button in Spotify's top bar. Browse public rooms, join a private one with its code, or create your own. As host, click Make DJ next to someone to let them add songs to the queue, play playlists, skip and pause; they can also right-click any song or playlist in Spotify. Everything a DJ does plays on the host's Spotify, so everyone hears it. Everyone plays the music on their own Spotify account; Spectra only shares what's playing and where, and keeps everyone within a couple of seconds of the host. Rooms close when the host ends them, or six hours after the last activity.

Do I need Spotify Premium?

No. Free accounts work. Some extensions depend on Premium-only features, and those say so themselves.

Why did my theme look wrong after a Spotify update?

Spotify renamed parts of its interface. Spectra rebuilds its list of names automatically and pushes fixes from its update server, so give it a minute, or reload Spotify. If it's still off, the theme itself may need an update from its author.

Does it work on phones?

Not right now. Spotify's phone apps are built in a way themes can't reach. The Meta Quest version works because it runs Spotify's web player.

How do I uninstall it?

Browser: remove the extension like any other. Windows and Linux: turn off "Always open Spotify with Spectra" first so Spotify's shortcuts go back to normal, then uninstall it (Settings → Apps on Windows). Mac: quit Spectra from the menu bar and drag it to the Bin. Quest: uninstall it from your library.
